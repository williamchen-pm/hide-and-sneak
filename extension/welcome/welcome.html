Welcome to Hide & Sneak

Let browser AI agents, and anyone watching your screen share, see only what they need. Three quick steps, about a minute.

1 Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon to the right of the address bar, then click the pin next to Hide & Sneak. That's where you'll turn protection on and off.

2 How will you use it most?

You can switch any time from the popup.

3 See it work

This turns protection on and opens a test page full of fake sensitive information, so you can see what gets hidden. Turn it off afterward from the icon, or with Alt Shift H.
